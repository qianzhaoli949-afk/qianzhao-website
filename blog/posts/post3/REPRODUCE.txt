BLOG POST 3 — Qianzhao Li

Research question: How did unemployment differ by education before, during,
and after the pandemic among U.S. civilian adults ages 25–64?

1. Obtain an authorized IPUMS CPS extract at https://cps.ipums.org/.
   Choose Cross-sectional, Basic Monthly, every month January 2019–December
   2024 (72 samples). Do not add ASEC samples. Select AGE, EMPSTAT, EDUC,
   and WTFINL; YEAR and MONTH and technical identifiers are preselected.
   Select cases: AGE 25 through 64 inclusive, selected individuals only.
   Format: fixed-width .dat, rectangular. Download .dat.gz and DDI .xml.
   Keep the extract outside the website folder. Do not commit or redistribute it.

2. Install R and xml2 (install.packages("xml2")). The analysis uses base R
   plus xml2, so ipumsr is not required. Run from this post's folder:
   Rscript analyze.R /path/to/cps_00001.xml /path/to/cps_00001.dat.gz

   The script reads column locations and implied decimals from the DDI.
   It reads compressed fixed-width records in chunks and scales WTFINL by
   10^4 once. It saves only grouped totals and rates in summary.rds.
   It overwrites summary.rds and session-info.txt with the new results.

3. Recreate the three charts in R:
   s <- readRDS("summary.rds")
   source("plots.R")
   draw_figures(s)

4. To render in the website, install knitr and rmarkdown and use Quarto:
   quarto render blog/posts/post3/index.qmd
   Rendering uses the saved summary.rds, not the original microdata.

DEFINITIONS
Employed: EMPSTAT 10,12. Unemployed: 20,21,22. Labor force = both groups.
Other civilian statuses (30–36) are outside the unemployment denominator.
Exclude nonpositive weights, noncivilian/NIU statuses, missing education.
Education codes:
  Less than high school: 002,010,020,030,040,050,060,071
  High school diploma/equivalent: 073
  Some college/associate: 081,091,092
  Bachelor's or higher: 111,123,124,125
Missing/ambiguous education: 000,001,999. Unexpected codes stop the script.
Age bands: 25–34, 35–49, 50–64.

Monthly rate = 100 * sum(weight * unemployed) / sum(weight * labor force).
Annual rate = 100 * sum(monthly unemployment totals) / sum(monthly LF totals).
Dividing both annual sums by 12 gives the same rate. Raw annual totals in
summary.rds are person-month totals, not annual population counts.
Observations repeat across months and must not be treated as independent
unique people. No confidence intervals or significance tests are claimed.
All statistics are unadjusted for seasonality and not causal estimates.

VALIDATION
The script checks year/month ranges, fixed-width line lengths, DDI scaling,
missing fields, education mappings, 288 month-education cells, 24 annual cells,
72 year-education-age cells, sample accounting, and valid rate bounds.
The summary includes input MD5 checksums and sequential exclusion counts.
session-info.txt records the software environment. Future source revisions
may change estimates. The extraction date for this analysis is 2026-09-27.
